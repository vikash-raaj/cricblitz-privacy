Privacy Policy for CricBlitz

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CricBlitz ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application CricBlitz (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

CricBlitz is an offline cricket game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users.

The App stores game progress data (such as career stats, scores, and settings) locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us.

2. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET – Required by the React Native framework. The App does not use this permission to transmit any user data.
  • VIBRATE – Used solely to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. No data is collected through this permission.

3. Third-Party Services

CricBlitz does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Children's Privacy

CricBlitz does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for all ages.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any user data, there is no risk of your personal data being exposed through our App.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
